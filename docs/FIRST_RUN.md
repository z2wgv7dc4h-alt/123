## Coach (idle)

> Hit **1 · Generate** — or drop your track above first (optional).

Play and Export stay **locked** until you Generate (`HELP.playDisabled` / `HELP.exportDisabled`).

## Three beats (Simple spine)

1. **Generate** (`G`) — What: new drum & bass sketch. When: click anytime. What happens: original near 174 BPM (not a clone).
2. **Play** (`Space`) — What: hear the mix. When: after Generate. What happens: listen; mute updates Play live — **rehear** with Play again (no Generate for stems).
3. **Export ZIP** (`E`) — What: ZIP for your DAW. When: after you like it. What happens: dry tracks + MIDI; remixed Play adds `mix_as_heard.wav`. Optional: **Download what I heard** = one preview WAV.

### Beat 0 (optional)

**Style Ref** — What: vibe from a file you own. When: drop before Generate (or skip). What happens: biases mood; never clones; ~174 stays.

## After Generate

- **Again** = same seed + current knobs · **Vary** = new seed.
- **Live now (mid-play):** mute/solo/gain only — audible without remux-on-Play (`HELP.paramsVsMixer`). Export still builds `mix_as_heard` when dirty.
- **Next Generate:** energy / darkness / chaos / BPM / bars / style text / seed — not live.
- More: Surprise Me + Favorites.
