# Why ~174 BPM? — retired

**Retired 2026-09-17** (`1b3a702` B-1, `c9c878f` B-2).

Tempo is no longer locked. It is a setting (70–200, `clampProductBpm` in
`src/core/types/index.ts`). Picking a genre sets its default tempo:

| Genre | Default BPM |
|---|---|
| Drum & bass | 174 |
| Dubstep | 140 |
| Half-time | 170 (half-time drums) |
| Jungle | 165 |

Presets carry their own genre and tempo. Sketch's real break loops are cut at
174, so they switch off more than 6 BPM away from it.
