# R-4 Duplicate / move sections with seam repaint

**Status: Merged into E-1.**

- Duplicate: splice the selected section's audio after itself at bar lines.
- Move: cut and reinsert at a bar line.
- Then repaint a short window (about 1 bar each side) over every new seam.
- Middle-of-song "Extend +N" = duplicate the last N bars of the section + seam repaint.

Done when: splice tests prove exact sample counts at bar lines, one seam repaint
window is sent per seam, and Undo restores the previous take.
