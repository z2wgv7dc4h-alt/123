`src/ui/hooks/useStudioStore.ts`, `src/ui/components/StyleDropZone.tsx`.

**Change**: Full spec already researched and written up in
`docs/HANDOFF.md` §3 item 1 — **read that section before starting, it has

from `C:\Users\RIGGUSPIG\Documents\ACE-Step-1.5\docs\en\API.md` (the real,

automatically skipped for `cover` — don't spend time tuning captions for
this path.

behavior — it's unrelated and works. Do not ship the technical change
without rewriting `StyleDropZone.tsx`'s "Not a clone" / "we bias mood and
energy of an original sketch" copy — real audio-conditioned generation
resembles the reference more than a 3-number nudge does, and shipping the
old copy alongside the new behavior would be dishonest, not just stale.

audio that's audibly conditioned on that file's actual sound (not just its
