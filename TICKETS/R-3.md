# R-3 Downbeat detection — bar grid aligned to the take

**Status: Open.** After R-2.

ACE output is at the requested BPM, but bar 1 may not start at sample 0. Detect
the first downbeat offset (low-band onset energy) and the effective tempo, store
them on the take, and use them for every bar↔seconds conversion in R-2 / R-4.
`src/core/audio/onsetGrid.ts` is the starting point.

Done when: synthetic fixture tests at 140 and 174 with a known offset recover
the offset within ±10 ms, and Redo/Extend use the offset.
