# P-1 Prompt v2 — paragraph captions + structure-tag lyrics + longer songs

**Status: Open** (paste issued 2026-09-17).

Why: ACE's shipped examples use 60–110 word paragraph captions and structure-tag
lyrics (see `docs/ACE-NOTES.md` "Captions and lyrics"). Our comma-tag caption had
duplicates, and our `[Instrumental]`-only lyrics removed the build/drop timeline
because of a wrong reading of `is_instrumental()`.

- `buildAceCaption` composes sentences: genre + energy (or section role), drums + bass,
  arrangement narrative from the song map, extras (user words / layers / shape, deduped),
  "polished and club-ready, with no vocals". Vocabulary helpers stay.
- `buildAceLyrics(sectionNames)`: `[Intro - atmospheric]`, `[Build - rising tension]`,
  `[Drop - explosive]`, `[Breakdown - stripped back]`, `[Outro - fade out]`.
- Bridge sends the browser's lyrics, keeping only `[...]` lines; `[Instrumental]` fallback.
- Default 96 bars; caps 128 bars (store, structureEdit, song map) and 480 s (bridge).
