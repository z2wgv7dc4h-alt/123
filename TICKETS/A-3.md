# A-3 Tempo blocks (real tempo changes)

**Status: Open.** After A-2.

ACE renders one BPM per call. A block with a different tempo is rendered separately at its
BPM with the previous block as ACE `reference_audio` (palette continuity), then joined with
a transition (the build ends on a riser/stop → impact → new tempo). Hard cut, no tempo ramp.
The structure and song map carry per-block tempo; bar↔seconds math becomes per block.
